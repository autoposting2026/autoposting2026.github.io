FAQ

What exactly does AutoSEO do?
AutoSEO automates your entire content strategy. It finds low-competition keywords, writes highly optimized articles mimicking your brand voice, and publishes them directly to your WordPress website daily while you sleep.
How many articles can I generate per month?
Our system is designed to provide unlimited content ideas and automatically publish daily, giving you a steady stream of high-quality, SEO-optimized articles every month on autopilot.
Can I connect AutoSEO to my existing website?
Yes! We are built specifically for WordPress. You can connect your site securely in seconds using WordPress Application Passwords, allowing us to push generated articles directly to your blog as drafts or published posts.
Does AutoSEO handle technical SEO?
AutoSEO focuses entirely on generating top-ranking content and on-page SEO (like H1/H2 tags and keyword density). It does not alter your site's backend code or server architecture.
What languages does AutoSEO support?
We support over 50+ languages, allowing you to easily expand your audience with globally-optimized articles. Our built-in site translator also helps users read your site natively!
Can I use my brand voice or custom tone?
Absolutely. AutoSEO automatically scans your existing website to capture your exact style, tone, and audience. Every article sounds personalized for your brand.
Do I need any SEO knowledge to use AutoSEO?
None at all. Easy setup, zero work. If you know your website's URL, you have all the knowledge needed. We handle the keyword research, difficulty analysis, and publishing.
Does AutoSEO generate images for articles?
Yes, we can automatically pair your blog posts with relevant, high-quality images so they are visually engaging and ready to rank immediately.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
No. AutoSEO is a month-to-month service. You can cancel anytime directly from your dashboard, and you keep all the content we've already generated for you.
What makes AutoSEO different from other SEO tools?
Most tools (DIY) just give you data and make you do the work. AutoSEO is a true autopilot—it finds the keywords, writes the content, AND publishes it for you, replacing a $5,000/month agency for just €31/mo (about €1 a day).
